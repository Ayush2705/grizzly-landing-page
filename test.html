Safe Rides, Bright Futures: Trusted School Transport You Can Count On.

Grizzly Public School offers convenient, safe, and reliable transportation with both AC and Non-AC school buses and pickup vans. Our transportation ensures a comfortable, hassle-free journey to school. With a focus on safety and punctuality, our well-maintained vehicles provide students with a secure and pleasant commute, allowing them to focus on their studies and activities without worrying about travel.

At Grizzly Public School, our Co-Curricular Activities (CCA) program is a cornerstone of talent development, offering house-wise activities every Thursday. These begin with a class round for all students, followed by a final round in the next week. To ensure equal opportunities, materials are provided by the school for class-level activities. Students receive guidance from dedicated house teachers and members in diverse activities such as debates, solo singing, calligraphy, art, and quizzes. The program fosters teamwork, creativity, and healthy competition, inspiring students to achieve excellence through special prizes and merit certificates, transforming their raw talents into polished skills.
