Engage, Explore, Excel: Our Vibrant Activities

THEME BASED ASSEMBLY

Our Theme-Based Assembly is a unique initiative where every student has the opportunity to perform and showcase their talents around a specific theme. From illustrating the Parts of a Plant to displaying Art and Craft work, students actively contribute to the assembly, enriching the learning experience. The assembly agenda is thoughtfully organized by one section of students, and the responsibility rotates, allowing everyone to participate. This collaborative approach fosters creativity, teamwork, and a deeper understanding of diverse topics.

MODERN TEACHING AIDS WITH 3D EFFECT

At Grizzly Public School, we prioritize the use of Teaching Learning Materials (TLM) to create vibrant, student-centered learning environments. Our TLMs include visual aids, models, digital resources, and tools designed for hands-on learning experiences, ensuring that every concept is delivered in a creative and engaging way. With a focus on quality and ample quantity, our resources cater to diverse learning styles, making lessons interactive and impactful. Special tools for developing fine motor skills further enhance learning. At Grizzly, we believe in fostering creativity, critical thinking, and a love for learning that prepares students for the future.

EXPERIENTIAL LEARNING TOUR

Our Experiential Learning Tour offers students the opportunity to explore real-world settings and gain firsthand knowledge beyond the classroom. Students visit a variety of locations, including a police station, old age homes, bank, post office, court, iron plant, rice mill, and the Cobra Military Unit. The program also includes exciting outstation tours to destinations like Shimla, Nainital, and Rajasthan, along with camping and visits to historic places. These immersive experiences enhance practical learning, foster curiosity, and help students develop a broader perspective on society, history, and industry, while promoting teamwork and personal growth.

ENGLISH DRAMA CLUB

At Grizzly Public School, the English Drama Club offers students an exciting platform to bring literature to life. Starting from Class 1, and guided by our expert English teachers, students prepare and perform dramas and skits based on chapters from their textbooks. This engaging activity enhances their understanding of the content while fostering creativity, teamwork, and effective communication. By enacting scenes, students build confidence, refine their language skills, and experience the joy of collaboration. The English Drama Club transforms learning into a dynamic, interactive, and memorable journey, inspiring students to express themselves with flair and enthusiasm.

PUPPET THEATER

At Grizzly Public School, the Puppet Theater is a magical haven where stories come alive and creativity knows no bounds! This enchanting space allows students to delve into the art of imaginative puppetry, fostering communication, teamwork, and self-expression. Through interactive storytelling, students enhance their narrative skills, spark boundless imagination, and bring their ideas to life in the most delightful way. Guided by trained mentors, every puppet becomes a voice, every performance a lesson, and every story an unforgettable experience. At Grizzly, we nurture creativity, one puppet story at a time!

NEP AND NCF

At Grizzly Public School, our curriculum is thoughtfully aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP) and the National Curriculum Framework (NCF), offering students a holistic and future-ready education. Our NEP-inspired approach emphasizes experiential learning, critical thinking, and multidisciplinary growth, supported by NCF-aligned textbooks and innovative resources. From hands-on experiments to creative arts and sports activities, we integrate academics with skill-building to foster well-rounded development. At Grizzly, we prepare students to excel in academics, explore their potential, and thrive in an evolving world—empowering them to lead with confidence and purpose.
