Where Learning Becomes Adventure!

At Grizzly Public School, learning is not just a process — it's an adventure. Guided by the belief that "Education is life itself," we create a dynamic environment where curiosity, creativity, and exploration thrive. Our state-of-the-art infrastructure and innovative tools transform every lesson into an exciting journey — from hands-on experiments to artistic expression and cultural performances. Beyond academics, our sports events and personality development programs inspire confidence, leadership, and growth. At Grizzly, every child is encouraged to embrace challenges, "chart their own path," and become a pioneer of tomorrow's world!

Our Goals

To impart quality education along with moral values to face this competitive world.

To develop qualities of leadership, self-respect, and human values.

To inculcate discipline, self-dependence, excellent character, and respect for all.

To produce ideal citizens with excellent competence, high moral values, and commitment to society and the nation.

Our Mission

Empowering every student with the skills and knowledge required to cope with the competitive challenges of the complex world.

Strengthen collaboration between the school, families, and community.

Create a safe and supportive environment.

Challenge students to become lifelong learners and productive, responsible citizens.

Our Vision

To emerge as one of the best schools in the state (Jharkhand) in the coming years.

Messages from Leadership

MR. AVINASH SETH

DIRECTOR

At Grizzly, we attempt to create an environment which is conducive to learning so that all children feel able and encouraged to become successful learners. Life is now a vicious competition and change is inevitable. Understanding this, the school is conscious that mere intellectual growth is not enough to excel in life. Thus, the overall school program aims to give the students maximum exposure and inculcates in them the skills and values that will help them to face challenges in future. Many of our students have excelled in Academics, Sports, and other extra-curricular activities at all levels and have brought laurels to the school. We are dedicated to fostering a love of learning, promoting critical thinking, and encouraging creativity and innovation. Our committed and highly qualified educators are passionate about providing engaging and student-centred instruction, ensuring that every student's unique talent and strength are recognized and nurtured. As the School Director, my commitment is to create a safe, inclusive, and stimulating learning environment where every student feels valued, supported, and inspired to excel. I encourage open communication and collaboration between students, parents, and staff, as we believe that strong partnerships are essential for the success of our school community.

MR. MANISH KAPSIME

DIRECTOR

The first step into school is that giant step forward where children start discovering the wide world outside and the depth of the world within. As we hold the little finger and guide the young ones forward, we realize the responsibility of nurturing this curiosity igniting their minds to fly high in the sky. We aim to create in our students a cutting edge which will bring success in the competitive world. Children are encouraged to explore and rejoice in the joy of learning. Rote learning is discouraged and discussion based learning is promoted, where each student is guided through a work plan according to his/her potential. We believe that the purpose of education is to replace an empty mind with an open one. Our faculty and administration are focused on providing a healthy learning experience for all students through various curricular and extracurricular activities available.

The Study of Flexibility

(Aligned with NEP and NCF)

At Grizzly Public School, we believe in the power of flexibility to shape each student's unique journey. Our curriculum, aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP) and the National Curriculum Framework (NCF), encourages curiosity, experiential learning, and critical thinking. Through self-created activity notebooks and innovative resources, we foster a hands-on, multidisciplinary approach that supports both academic and personal growth. Beyond academics, students explore a wide range of activities—from literary, scientific, and cultural events to sports and personality development programs. With state-of-the-art infrastructure and dedicated mentors, we empower students to become confident, purpose-driven leaders, ready to make an impact on the world.

Shaping Tomorrow's Leaders

At Grizzly Public School, we believe "Education is life itself." Guided by the idea that "One book, one pen, one child, and one teacher can change the world," we empower students to unlock their potential and impact the future. Our innovative approach focuses on scholastic and co-scholastic development, ensuring holistic growth — intellectually, emotionally, and socially.

In a short span, we have carved a unique niche with state-of-the-art infrastructure, advanced study materials, and a curriculum that sparks curiosity. Beyond academics, we offer a wide range of activities like literary, scientific, cultural, artistic, and sports events, along with personality development programs that foster self-reliance, confidence, and leadership.

At Grizzly Public School, every child is treated as an individual, with a focus on nurturing diverse talents and values. Our dedicated teachers are mentors, guiding students toward excellence and preparing them to lead tomorrow with purpose and integrity.
