Jhumri Telaiya: Where Joy Meets Adventure

Local Flavor and Beyond Aug 12, 2020

Beyond the club gates lies the vibrant town of Jhumri Telaiya—a microcosm of Jharkhand's soul. Here's what awaits you

Local Delicacies Explore the bustling marketplaces. Savor litti-chokha—a traditional dish that warms the heart. Chat with street vendors selling colorful bangles and handwoven textiles. Jhumri Telaiya's flavors linger on your taste buds, connecting you to its roots.

Outdoor Escapades Hike to Pahari Baba Temple, perched atop a hill. The panoramic view of the valley rewards your effort. Or venture deeper into the forests, where ancient trees guard secrets. Listen to the rustle of leaves—the whispers of generations past.

Historical Gems Visit the old railway station—the echo of steam engines still resonates. Explore abandoned mines—their tunnels tell tales of labor and resilience. Jhumri Telaiya's history unfolds as you walk its streets, tracing the footsteps of those who shaped it.
